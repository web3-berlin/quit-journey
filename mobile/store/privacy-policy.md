# Quit Journey privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Quit Journey is an addiction-recovery app. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — what you are quitting, your start date and usage habits, cravings, slips, daily check-ins (including mood, sleep, weight and withdrawal symptoms), rewards, quit aids, support contacts and notes — is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

## What we collect

Nothing. Quit Journey has no account, no server of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries. We never receive, sell or share your data.

## Backups

When you tap **Save a backup**, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where that file goes (for example your own cloud drive or email). That transfer happens only when you ask for it, and the file is then handled by the app or service you chose.

## Notifications

If you turn on notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device by Android. No data leaves the phone.

## Phone calls and messages

Tapping a support contact or the quitline opens your phone's dialer. Message buttons open your messaging app with a prefilled text; you decide whether to send it. The app does not make, send, read or record calls or messages.

## Location

If you save risky places, the app can check your position **while it is open** to warn you when you are near one. It asks for Android's location permission first, never uses location in the background, and keeps your places and position on the device.

## Screen time

For gaming or social-media journeys you can give the app Android's *usage access* so it can show how many minutes you spent today in the apps you choose. That information is read and shown on the device only.

## Meeting lists

If you enter the address of a meeting feed (for example from your local AA or NA intergroup), the app downloads that public list from the address you entered. The request contains nothing about you beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address, seen by that server). Starred meetings are stored on the device.

## PIN and disguised icon

An optional PIN is stored on the device as a salted hash, never in plain text. The optional "Notes" icon only changes how the app appears on your home screen.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported are not affected; delete them wherever you saved them.

## Children

Quit Journey is intended for adults (18+).
