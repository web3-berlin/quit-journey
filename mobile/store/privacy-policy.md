# Quit Journey privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 October 2026_

Quit Journey is an addiction-recovery app. This policy explains what happens to the information you enter.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter — what you are quitting, your start date and usage habits, cravings, slips, daily check-ins (including mood, sleep, weight and withdrawal symptoms), rewards, quit aids, support contacts and notes — is stored **only on your device**, in the app's private storage.

## What we collect

Nothing. Quit Journey has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking libraries. We never receive, sell or share your data.

## Backups

When you tap **Save a backup**, the app creates a file and opens Android's share sheet. You choose where that file goes (for example your own cloud drive or email). That transfer happens only when you ask for it, and the file is then handled by the app or service you chose.

## Notifications

If you turn on notifications, reminders are scheduled locally on your device by Android. No data leaves the phone.

## Phone calls

Tapping a support contact or the quitline opens your phone's dialer. The app does not make or record calls.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes everything it stored. Backup files you exported are not affected; delete them wherever you saved them.

## Children

Quit Journey is intended for adults (18+).
