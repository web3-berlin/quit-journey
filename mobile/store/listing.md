**App name** (max 30): Quit Journey: Stop Smoking

**Short description** (max 80):

**Full description** (max 4000):

Quit Journey turns every smoke-free minute into something you can see.

Watch the sky clear
Your smoke-free clock ticks over a sky whose haze lifts as your body recovers — from your heart rate settling after 20 minutes to your lung cancer risk halving after ten years. Every milestone is based on guidance from the WHO, CDC, American Cancer Society and NHS, with a live countdown to the next one.

Beat cravings with what actually works for you
Tap SOS when a craving hits: a guided 4-7-8 breathing exercise, a countdown that shows the craving fading, your own reasons for quitting, and one tap to call someone who's got your back. Log how it went, and Quit Journey learns which strategies work best for you, which triggers hit hardest and which hours are your riskiest — then warns you before they come round again.

See the money pile up
Money saved, cigarettes not smoked and life expectancy regained, updated every second. Set rewards — a dinner out, new trainers, a weekend away — and claim them when your savings jar covers the cost.

Level up
Grow from Seed to Ancient Forest with XP for every smoke-free day, every craving you beat and every daily check-in. Unlock 31 badges, from Day One to Smoke-Free Legend, plus Iron Will, Comeback Kid and Full Toolbox.

Slips without shame
A slip doesn't erase your progress. Your quit date stays, your savings adjust honestly, and if you need a fresh start your past attempts stay in your history.

The full program
• Prep mode with a countdown, a readiness checklist and a gentle cut-down plan
• Daily check-ins for mood, cravings, energy, sleep, weight and withdrawal symptoms
• Quit-aid tracker for patches, gum, lozenges, varenicline and more
• Support contacts and your local quitline, one tap away
• Reminders for check-ins and health milestones

Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your journey is stored only on your phone. Save a backup file whenever you like and restore it on a new phone.

Quit Journey supports your decision to quit; it isn't medical advice. Talk to a doctor or pharmacist about quit aids and medication.
