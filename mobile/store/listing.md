# Play Store listing

**App name** (max 30): Quit Journey: Addiction Recovery

**Short description** (max 80):
Quit or cut down anything: recovery timeline, savings, badges and a craving SOS.

**Full description** (max 4000):

Quit Journey turns every day of recovery into something you can see — whether you're leaving behind cigarettes, alcohol, cannabis, opioids, stimulants, benzodiazepines, gambling, gaming, porn, social media, caffeine, sugar or something else.

Watch the sky clear
Your clock ticks over a sky whose haze lifts as you recover. Each addiction gets its own recovery timeline with sources: the 20-minute heart-rate drop after a last cigarette, the liver and blood-pressure gains of a month without alcohol, cannabinoid receptors resetting after about four weeks, dopamine recovery after stimulants, and milestones in money, time and habit for behavioural addictions.

Quit completely — or cut down first
Choose abstinence or a daily limit. Cutting down is a real goal here: days at or under your limit count as wins, and the app tracks your streak, savings and progress honestly.

Safety first, without judgement
For opioids and benzodiazepines, the app explains why tolerance drops after a break and how to stay alive if you use again: never alone, use less, carry naloxone. For alcohol and benzodiazepines, it warns clearly when stopping suddenly needs a doctor. A slip never erases your progress.

Beat cravings with what actually works for you
Tap SOS when a craving hits: a guided 4-7-8 breathing exercise, a countdown that shows the craving fading, your own reasons for quitting, and one tap to call someone who's got your back or your helpline. Quit Journey learns which strategies work best for you, which triggers hit hardest and which hours are your riskiest — and warns you before they come round again.

See what you're getting back
Money saved, units skipped, hours given back to your day and (for smokers) life expectancy regained, updated every second. Set rewards and claim them when your savings jar covers the cost.

Level up
Grow from Seed to Ancient Forest with XP for every day on track, every craving you beat and every daily check-in. Unlock 31 badges.

More than one addiction?
Run a separate journey for each, side by side, with reminders for all of them.

The full program
• Prep mode with a countdown, a readiness checklist and a gentle cut-down plan
• Daily check-ins for mood, cravings, energy, sleep, weight and withdrawal symptoms
• Medication and support tracker: nicotine replacement, methadone, buprenorphine, naltrexone, acamprosate, support groups, therapy
• Support contacts and your helpline, one tap away
• Reminders for check-ins and recovery milestones

Private by design
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your journeys are stored only on your phone. Save a backup file whenever you like.

Quit Journey supports your recovery; it isn't medical advice or treatment. Talk to a doctor about medication and before stopping alcohol or benzodiazepines after heavy use. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.
