# Security policy

Quit Journey stores sensitive health information on people's phones. Please report vulnerabilities privately.

- **Email:** frank@web3.berlin, or use GitHub's _Report a vulnerability_ (Security tab).
- Include steps to reproduce and the affected version or commit.
- Please don't open a public issue for security problems.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 working days.

Out of scope: forks and rebuilt copies of the app published by third parties.
